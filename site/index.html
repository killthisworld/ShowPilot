TEMPLATES

Fill it in once. Use it on every show.

Your act, its members, inputs and stage plot, or your venue's details, live in My Templates. From then on, a booking or an invite starts filled in.

  1. 01 · MAKE IT ONCE

    Build your template

    Add who's in the act and what they play, place them on the stage, and save. Venues save the room: Wi-Fi, power, console and contacts.

  2. 02 · BOOK WITH IT

    Book a date, already filled in

    Book a date fills itself from your template: the act, its picture and genres, and your stage plot and inputs in the tech rider.

  3. 03 · INVITED? LOAD IT

    Join an event in one tap

    When someone invites you to their event, tap Load, pick your template, and your part of the event is filled in.

HOW IT WORKS

From booked to show day in three steps.

  1. 01 · BOARDING

    Make the event

    Date, times, venue and who's playing. Show Pilot gives the night its own page.

  2. 02 · EN ROUTE

    Send each person a link

    The venue, the acts and the techs open their link, load their template, and their part is done.

  3. 03 · LANDED

    Everyone sees what's left

    The flight log shows how ready each profile is, with tasks and chat rooms to sort out the rest.
