TEMPLATES

Fill it in once. Use it on every show.

Your act, its members, inputs and stage plot, or your venue's details, live in My Templates. From then on, a booking or an invite starts filled in.

  1. 01 · MAKE IT ONCE

    Build your template

    Your act, its members and stage plot, your contact, advance and travel, each a section you fill in once. Venues save the room: Wi-Fi, power, console and contacts.

  2. 02 · BOOK WITH IT

    Book a date, already filled in

    Book a date opens as a web around your act, already filled in from your template: your contact, and your stage plot and inputs in the tech rider.

  3. 03 · INVITED? LOAD IT

    Join an event in one tap

    When someone invites you to their event, tap Load Template, pick yours, and your part of the event is filled in.

HOW IT WORKS

From booked to show day in three steps.

  1. 01 · BOARDING

    Make the event

    Date, times, venue and who's playing. Show Pilot gives the night its own page.

  2. 02 · EN ROUTE

    Send each person a link

    The venue, the acts and the techs open their link, load their template, and their part is done.

  3. 03 · LANDED

    Everyone sees what's left

    The flight log shows how ready each profile is, with tasks and chat rooms to sort out the rest.

OUR STORY

How it started

Founded by two brothers who kept experiencing the same problem when running sound for live events. Out of the chaos of long, cluttered email threads between venues and artist management, and the inconsistency of receiving information such as a simple stage plot, the intake form feature and the interactive stage plot were born.

Since then, ShowPilot has proven time after time to provide a level of efficiency between all parties of live events that has been long awaited in this industry.

We thank God for this beautiful opportunity to be a part of a revolutionizing system, and we thank you for trusting ShowPilot with your event.
