HOW IT WORKS

From booked to show day in three steps.

  1. 01 · BOARDING

    Make the event

    Date, times, venue and who's playing. Show Pilot gives the night its own page.

  2. 02 · EN ROUTE

    Send each person a link

    The venue, the acts and the techs open their link and join their part of the event.

  3. 03 · LANDED

    Everyone fills in their part

    Load-in, set times, stage plots and deal terms land on the same page, with tasks and chat rooms to sort out what's left.
